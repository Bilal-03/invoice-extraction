# Invoice Intelligence brand

Invoice Intelligence is an accounts-payable operations console: it turns invoice documents into an evidence-led ledger for extraction, validation, human review, approvals, vendor controls, and payment visibility.

## Core mark

The mark is a folded invoice carrying an upward evidence path. The invoice makes the product category legible; the path represents extraction signals becoming clearer through verification and workflow control. The amber endpoint is a small attention cue for review and decision-making.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink / forest | `#20251F` | Sidebar, icon field, primary actions, body ink |
| Paper | `#F1F0EA` | App canvas, invoice sheet, warm negative space |
| Ledger teal | `#24705D` | Evidence path, healthy states, positive workflow cues |
| Review amber | `#B4792B` | Attention, due-soon and review cues |
| Soft ink | `#62685F` | Secondary text and supporting labels |

## Usage

Keep the mark optically clear at small sizes. Do not add a drop shadow, bevel, gradient, extra initials, currency symbols, or a generic checkmark. Keep at least one quarter of the mark’s width as clear space around it when it is placed beside other elements.
